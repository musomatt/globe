The day map is unchanged apart from its filename. Normal and specular maps were converted from TIFF through PNG to lossless WebP for browser use, preserving the original 8192 × 4096 dimensions. The specular map is used as a water mask. The scene's shaders reinterpret the day map as a cool charcoal material and apply custom lighting; this is an artistic treatment, not a natural-colour or live image of Earth. No endorsement by the original creators is implied.

The 2048 × 1024 cloud map is unchanged apart from its filename. It supplies opacity and faint surface shadows for an independently drifting cloud shell. It is a static artistic texture, not live weather data.

The stars and satellite-like particles are generated by this application. They do not depict tracked objects or real orbital data.
